Return Filing Dashboard

Manage regular and correction returns with filing details, due dates and status in one clear view.

  • Filter by financial year and form number.
  • Track filing dates, due dates and return status.
  • Review token numbers and correction revisions.
  • Access e-Filing and TRACES from the dashboard.

TaxPower TDS
Computation & Compliance

Review tax calculations and compliance checks together before moving ahead with your return.

  • View tax payable and challan utilisation.
  • Check invalid and inoperative PANs.
  • Identify unverified or mismatched challans.
  • Review short deductions and missing salary details.
  • Track interest, late fees and outstanding balances.

TaxPower TDS
Return Register

Track returns across deductors, review processing progress and keep filing records close at hand.

  • Filter by group, period, form and return type.
  • View deductor details, ARN and token numbers.
  • Track filing dates and return status.
  • Review processing and certificate availability.
  • Get return status and export the register.
